Privacy policy

Here’s what happens to your information when you use KabirUI. We don’t sell it, and the app has no advertising trackers or analytics SDKs.

What stays on your phone

Your home layout, preferences and optional app-usage suggestions are stored or processed locally. The current app has no account system or analytics SDK. We don’t receive a copy of your installed-app list, contacts, notification contents, selected files or usage history.

On Android 9 and newer, KabirUI’s optional Android cloud backup keeps launcher settings but excludes wallpaper image files to stay within Android’s backup size limit. Direct device-to-device transfers still include them. Use KabirUI’s manual backup file when you want to carry Home Modes, wallpaper images and custom icons yourself. On Android 7 and 8, system backup follows Android’s standard rules and size limit.

Permissions are for features

Usage Access helps suggest apps. If you pick a contact in Quick Search, Android’s contact picker gives KabirUI only that contact’s number to call or message; KabirUI doesn’t read or store your contact list. Notification Access enables badges and local media controls; notification text and artwork are not sent to KabirUI's web services. File search uses folders you select through Android. You can revoke access in Android settings. Uninstalling KabirUI removes its app-private settings, cached images, and downloads. Android may keep the wallpaper image already applied to the system wallpaper store.

Online features

Weather and city search send the city query or selected city’s coordinates to Open-Meteo. Web suggestions send search text to Google; opening Google or Play Store search shares your query with those services. News loads Google News RSS. The launcher also checks KabirUI’s public version manifest and loads wallpaper previews and downloads from KabirUI’s website. Those version and wallpaper requests do not include your home layout, installed-app list, contacts, or usage history. Network providers can receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, and follow their own policies.

This website and wallpaper downloads

Our own pages do not include analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Our pages use locally hosted Inter and JetBrains Mono, so font requests stay on this site and do not contact Google Fonts. Their Inter and JetBrains Mono licenses are available here. The support section links to Ko-fi but does not embed it. If you choose to visit Ko-fi or donate, Ko-fi receives your IP address and browser information and may use cookies under its own privacy policy. This site is hosted and delivered by Vercel. Vercel may process end-user IP addresses and system configuration information as described in its Privacy Notice. Loading pages and wallpaper previews sends normal web requests to the hosting provider.

Donations and messages

Ko-fi handles donations under its own policies. We don’t receive your card details. If you contact us or donate, the information you choose to share through that service may be visible to us. You can reach Kabir Invents through our Ko-fi page.

Changes

If our data practices change, we’ll update this page and its date. This policy describes the current pre-release app and website.
