Delete your data

KabirUI has no account system. You do not create an account to use it, and we do not keep an account or a copy of your home layout, installed-app list, contacts, notification contents, or usage history on our servers.

Delete data on your phone

Your home layout, preferences, and other launcher data are stored on your phone. To delete them, uninstall KabirUI, or go to Android Settings > Apps > KabirUI > Storage > Clear data. Uninstalling removes KabirUI's app-private settings, cached images, and downloads. Android may keep a wallpaper image you already applied to the system wallpaper store.

Ask us to delete anything we hold

If you contacted us or donated, we may hold only the information you chose to share through that service. To ask us to delete it, email kabirgagnejayt@gmail.com with "Delete my data" in the subject line and tell us what to delete. We will delete it and confirm by email.
